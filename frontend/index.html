// 02 — HOW IT SEES

Two models.
One verdict.

  1. 01

    You upload

    An image, a short video or a block of text. Nothing is stored: it is analyzed and deleted.

  2. 02

    Two detectors scan it

    Independent open-source models each estimate how likely it is to be AI. Video is sampled frame by frame.

  3. 03

    Scores are fused

    The estimates are calibrated and averaged into one verdict and a confidence score.

AUTHENTECH

Results are probabilistic estimates, not proof. Detectors can be wrong, especially on short text, edited media and newer AI models. Never use a single result to accuse anyone.
